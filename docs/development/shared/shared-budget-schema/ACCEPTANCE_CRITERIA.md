# Acceptance Criteria — F0-12 Budget buckets, fund top-ups, spending and summary calculation

Each criterion is observable and maps to at least one test in TEST_PLAN.md. Criteria may not be changed after implementation starts without a controlled change recorded in DECISIONS.md.

**Rewritten 2026-09-27** as CHG-020 / CHG-021 / CHG-022 require (see DECISIONS.md FD-01). Thresholds are 75 / 85 / 100 (PD-032). Overdraft no longer exists: pending costs replace it (PD-058). AC-01, AC-02, AC-04, AC-05 and AC-06 keep their original intent; AC-03 is replaced; AC-07 to AC-15 are new.

| ID | Story | Type | Criterion (Given / When / Then) | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AC-01 | US-01 | happy | Given a bucket with $24,000 in funds and $9,120 of paid costs, when the summary runs, then remaining is 14880.00 and percent_used is 38. | MET |
| AC-02 | US-01 | happy | Given a bucket with $3,000 in funds and $2,760 of paid costs, when the summary runs, then percent_used is 92 and threshold_state is 'alert'. Boundaries: 74% is 'normal', 75% 'warning', 85% 'alert', 100% 'depleted'. A bucket with $0 in funds has percent_used null and state 'normal'. | MET |
| AC-03 | US-01 | edge | Given a bucket with $500 remaining, when `remove_funds` asks for $500.01, then it raises "Only $500.00 available", nothing is inserted and remaining stays 500.00; asking for exactly $500.00 succeeds and leaves remaining 0.00. Remaining is never negative. | MET |
| AC-04 | US-01 | validation | Given `add_funds` or `remove_funds` is called with amount 0, -5, or 12.345, when executed, then it raises a validation error and nothing is inserted. | MET |
| AC-05 | US-01 | permission | Given a user not linked to Margaret, when they select Margaret's buckets, funds entries, costs or summary, then zero rows are returned. Margaret's family and her organisation's admins can change funds and buckets; an assigned carer can read but every change function raises 42501; another organisation's admin can neither read nor change. | MET |
| AC-06 | US-01 | validation | Given the decimal string '12.345', when parsed by the money schema, then validation fails (max 2 decimal places). Money is also refused when negative, zero (unless zero is allowed), non-numeric, or a float such as 1e3. | MET |
| AC-07 | US-02 | happy | Given an event with a $40 cost on a bucket with $100 remaining, when an occurrence is completed, then one cost of $40 is recorded as paid (paid_on today) and remaining is 60.00. Completing the same occurrence again after an undo charges nothing more. A recurring event is charged once per completed occurrence. | MET |
| AC-08 | US-02 | edge | Given an event with a $40 cost on a bucket with $25 remaining, when an occurrence is completed, then the occurrence is still Done, the whole $40 is recorded as pending (nothing part-paid), remaining stays 25.00, and the summary shows pending_total 40.00, pending_count 1 and state 'depleted'. While a bucket has any pending cost, a newer cost is also pending, even if the balance could cover it. | MET |
| AC-09 | US-02 | happy | Given a bucket with $0 remaining and pending costs of $40 (oldest), $10 and $30, when $45 is added, then only the $40 cost is paid (oldest first, whole) and remaining is 5.00; the $10 cost, which would fit a newer-first order, stays pending because settlement stops at the first cost the balance cannot cover. Adding $45 with pending costs of $60 (oldest) then $10 pays nothing (the $10 does not jump the $60). Each paid cost has paid_on set, keeps its own row, and no new row is added to History. Adding a further $5 then pays the $10 (remaining 0.00) and leaves the $30 pending. | MET |
| AC-10 | US-03 | validation | Given a client's buckets, when a bucket is added, renamed or funded, then: a name is required, at most 40 characters and unique for the client ignoring case and surrounding spaces (a violation raises 23505 or 22023); a starting amount of $0 is allowed and records "bucket_added" with amount 0; a starting amount above $0 records it; a rename records no history row and the bucket's entries and costs stay attached; kind, when given, is one of ndis, fixed, government. | MET |
| AC-11 | US-03 | edge | Given a bucket, when `remove_bucket` is called, then it succeeds only if no cost (paid or pending) was ever charged to it; the money left in it is recorded as a "bucket_removed" entry of minus that amount, remaining becomes 0.00 and the bucket no longer appears in the summary; events that pointed at it keep their cost and move to the client's "Miscellaneous" bucket (created with a $0 start, or reused if it exists) so future completions still cost; otherwise it raises 22023 and nothing changes. A removed bucket's name can be used again. | MET |
| AC-12 | US-02 | edge | Given a pending or paid cost whose event is later deactivated, when the summary and cost rows are read, then the cost is unchanged and still to be paid or paid; the cost's link to its event is `on delete set null`, so removing an event can never remove a cost. | MET |
| AC-13 | US-02 | edge | Given a pending cost, when any amount of time passes and funds are later added, then it is still pending until paid and is paid by that top-up: pending costs do not expire or reset. | MET |
| AC-14 | US-01 | permission | Given the budget tables, then each has row level security enabled, none can be written directly by an authenticated user, fund entries cannot be updated, deleted or truncated by anyone, a cost can change only from pending to paid (only status and paid_on), and every change is recorded by the F0-08 audit trigger. | MET |
| AC-15 | US-01 | happy | Given each change (funds, bucket, completion charge), then the row records who made it (`recorded_by`, display-name snapshot) and the save's optional note; the actor is always the signed-in user, never a parameter. | MET |

| AC-16 | US-02 | permission | Given a carer on an active shift for the client, when they create an event with a cost and a bucket, then it is saved, completing it charges that bucket, and another client's bucket is refused (22023). | MET |

Status values: NOT MET · MET (test passing) · BLOCKED (cite OQ/PD).
