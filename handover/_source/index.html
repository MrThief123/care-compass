Care Compass handover

Everything you need to put Care Compass online and to use it. Each guide has pictures of the real screens, written in plain language. Open a guide below, or use the menu at the top of every page.

Where to start: read the Setup guide first. It takes about one hour. After that, give each person the guide for their job: administrators, carers or family members.

Good to know

  • The pictures use made-up example people. No real client information is shown.
  • Click any picture to open it full size in a new tab.
  • To print a guide, use your browser's Print command. The menu is hidden on paper.
  • These pages are the same as the handover pages in Confluence, which stay up to date as the program changes.

The program

The Care Compass program is kept on GitHub: https://github.com/MrThief123/care-compass. Step 2 of the Setup guide shows how to make your own copy.
