Setting up your admin sign-in code

This guide is for organisation managers (Admin accounts) only. Family and carer accounts sign in with just an email and password and can skip it.

The pictures in this guide come from Care Compass itself. The square code in the pictures is marked "EXAMPLE ONLY". Don't scan it, because yours will be different.

Why admins need a code

An Admin account can see every client and staff member in the organisation, so it gets an extra lock. As well as your password, you type a 6-digit code from an app on your phone each time you sign in. Someone who learns your password still can't get in without your phone.

This is often called "two-factor authentication": the first factor is your password, and the second is your phone.

What you need

  • Your phone (an iPhone or an Android phone).
  • About 10 minutes.
  • Your Care Compass email and password.
  • The free Google Authenticator app. It shows a new 6-digit code every 30 seconds.

Part 1: install Google Authenticator

On an iPhone

  1. Open the App Store (the blue icon with a white "A").
  2. Tap Search at the bottom right and type Google Authenticator.
  3. Find the app made by Google LLC. Its icon is a grey and blue asterisk (*) shape.
  4. Tap Get, then confirm with Face ID, your fingerprint or your Apple password.
  5. When it has finished, tap Open.

On an Android phone

  1. Open the Google Play Store (the coloured triangle icon).
  2. Tap the search bar at the top and type Google Authenticator.
  3. Find the app made by Google LLC.
  4. Tap Install.
  5. When it has finished, tap Open.

The first time you open it

  1. Tap Get started.
  2. The app asks whether to sign in with a Google account. We recommend you sign in if you have a Gmail or Google account. Your codes are then backed up, so if you lose or replace your phone you can sign in to Google Authenticator on the new phone and your Care Compass code comes back. If you don't have a Google account, choose Use without an account. The app still works, but your codes live only on this phone.
  3. You now see a screen with two choices: Scan a QR code and Enter a setup key. Leave the app open here and go to your computer.

Part 2: link the app to Care Compass

You do this once, the first time you sign in to Care Compass as an admin.

  1. On your computer, open Care Compass and sign in with your email and password.
  2. A page called Set up two-factor authentication appears. It shows a black-and-white square pattern, called a QR code, and under it a line of letters and numbers labelled Manual entry key. Leave this page open.
  3. On your phone, in Google Authenticator, tap Scan a QR code. If you have added accounts to the app before, tap the coloured + button at the bottom right first.
  4. If the phone asks to use the camera, tap Allow or OK.
  5. Point the phone's camera at the square pattern on your computer screen and hold it steady. The app adds an entry called Care Compass with a 6-digit number under it. A small circle next to the number counts down; when it runs out, a new number appears.
  6. On your computer, type the 6-digit number into the 6-digit code box. Leave out the space in the middle.
  7. Click Verify and continue. You are taken to the Admin Home page.

The set-up is finished. Keep Google Authenticator on your phone, because you need it every time you sign in.

If the camera won't scan

Use the manual entry key instead:

  1. In Google Authenticator, tap Enter a setup key (tap the + button first if you don't see it).
  2. In Account name, type Care Compass.
  3. In Your key, type the letters and numbers shown next to Manual entry key on your computer screen, exactly as they appear.
  4. Leave Type of key as Time based.
  5. Tap Add, then carry on from step 6 above.

Part 3: signing in from then on

  1. Sign in with your email and password as usual.
  2. A page called Enter your authentication code appears.
  3. Open Google Authenticator on your phone and find the Care Compass entry.
  4. Type the 6-digit number into the 6-digit code box and click Verify.

If the countdown circle is nearly empty, wait for the new number before typing it.

If something goes wrong

What you seeWhat to do
"That code isn't right. Check your authenticator app and try again."The number probably changed while you were typing. Wait for a fresh number and enter it straight away. If Google Authenticator holds more than one entry, check you're reading the Care Compass one.
"Enter the 6-digit code."The box is empty, or what you typed isn't 6 numbers. Type just the 6 numbers, with no space.
"This setup has expired. Reload the page to start again."The set-up page was probably opened again in another tab or window. Close any other Care Compass tabs and refresh the page. A new square pattern appears. In Google Authenticator, delete the old Care Compass entry (press and hold it on Android, or swipe it left on iPhone), then scan the new pattern.
Codes keep being rejected even when typed quicklyYour phone's clock may be wrong. In your phone's settings, turn on the option to set the date and time automatically, then try again.

If you lose or replace your phone

  • New phone, and you signed in to Google Authenticator with your Google account: install Google Authenticator on the new phone (Part 1) and sign in with the same Google account. Your Care Compass entry appears by itself.
  • New phone, still have the old one, no Google account: in Google Authenticator on the old phone, open the menu (three dots or lines) and choose Transfer accounts, then Export accounts. It shows a QR code. On the new phone, install Google Authenticator, choose Transfer accounts, then Import accounts, and scan that code.
  • Phone lost or broken, no Google account: Care Compass has no "lost my phone" button and no backup codes. Contact the person who looks after the app's technical accounts. They can remove the old code from your Care Compass account. The next time you sign in you'll see the set-up page again and can follow Part 2 with your new phone.

Until then you can't open the Admin screens. Family and carer accounts are not affected.

Good habits

  • Don't share your codes or the square pattern with anyone. Each admin sets up Google Authenticator on their own phone.
  • Put a passcode, fingerprint or face lock on your phone.
  • If an admin leaves the organisation, deactivate their account in Care Compass. They can no longer sign in, even with a correct code.
